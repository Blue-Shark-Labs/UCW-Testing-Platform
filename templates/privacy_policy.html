Privacy Policy

Learn how Blue Shark Labs collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information.

1. Introduction

Blue Shark Labs provides an educational reinforcement testing platform designed to help students practise subject material through multiple-choice tests, monitor their academic progress and participate in subject leaderboards. This privacy policy explains how personal information is handled when using our platform.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your role and use of the platform, we may collect the following information:

  • Student number and account username.
  • First name and last name.
  • Course title and associated subjects.
  • Account credentials, including securely hashed passwords.
  • Test attempts, answers, scores, points and completion dates.
  • Academic progress, average scores and leaderboard information.
  • Account status, approval status and last login time.
  • Lecturer and administrator account information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to operate and maintain Blue Shark Labs, including to:

  • Create, authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Allow students to complete tests and view their results.
  • Calculate scores, points, averages and academic progress.
  • Display subject leaderboards.
  • Allow lecturers to manage student accounts and monitor progression.
  • Allow administrators to manage subjects and lecturer accounts.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent unauthorised access.
  • Maintain historical test results and academic records.

4. Passwords and Account Security

User passwords are not intended to be stored in plain text. The platform uses password hashing to protect stored credentials. Access to platform functionality is restricted according to user roles, such as student, lecturer and administrator.

Users should keep their login credentials confidential and contact Blue Shark Labs if they believe their account has been compromised.

5. Data Access and Sharing

Personal information is accessible only to users with appropriate permissions within the platform.

  • Students can view their own test results and progress.
  • Lecturers can manage student accounts and view student progression.
  • Administrators can manage subjects and lecturer accounts.

Personal information is not intended to be sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be made available where necessary to operate the platform, meet legal obligations or protect the security of the system.

6. Test Results and Leaderboards

Blue Shark Labs stores information about completed tests, including scores, percentages, points and completion dates, to support student progress tracking and lecturer monitoring.

Subject leaderboards may display student names and performance-related information to compare results between students. Users should be aware that their performance information may be visible to other participants through leaderboard functionality.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Personal information is stored within the platform's database and retained for as long as necessary to provide platform functionality, maintain academic records, support administration and meet applicable requirements.

Subjects may be marked as redundant rather than deleted to preserve historical academic records. Test results may also be retained where necessary for progress tracking and reporting.

Account deletion, disabling or retention is managed according to platform administration functionality and applicable data retention requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection legislation, users may have rights regarding their personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to their personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • The right to request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • The right to raise concerns about how their information is handled.

Requests relating to personal information should be directed to Blue Shark Labs using the contact details provided below.

9. Cookies and Session Data

Blue Shark Labs may use session cookies or similar technologies to maintain authenticated sessions, support navigation and protect account security. These technologies are used to support essential platform functionality.

Users should sign out after using the platform, particularly when accessing it from shared or public devices.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Blue Shark Labs' functionality, data handling practices or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, your personal information or how it is handled, please contact us using the details below.
